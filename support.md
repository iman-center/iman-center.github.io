# Support

Prayer and iqama times for the Al-Iman Center of Iowa.

## Contact

For questions about the app, corrections to prayer or iqama times, or problems on your
device, email **imancenterit@gmail.com**.

## Frequently asked

**Do I need an internet connection?**
No. Prayer time tables are built into the app, so athan times always display offline.
Iqama times refresh from the center's published schedule when a connection is
available and fall back to a bundled copy when it isn't.

**How do I add the home screen widget?**
Touch and hold an empty area of the home screen, tap the **+** button in the corner,
search for the app by name, choose the small, medium, or large widget, and tap
**Add Widget**.

**The iqama times look wrong.**
Iqama times are set by the center and can change. If what you see does not match the
schedule posted at the masjid, please email us so we can update it.

**Does the app use my location?**
No. Times are for the center's fixed location, and the app requests no location or
other device permissions.
