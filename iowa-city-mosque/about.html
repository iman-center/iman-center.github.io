About us

Al-Iman Center of Iowa

Also known as Masjid Al-Iman and Iowa City Mosque.

Our mission

What is our mission?

Al-Iman Center of Iowa, Inc.’s mission is grounded in the Qur’an and the Sunnah of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, upon the understanding of the righteous early generations (the Salaf), through knowledge, practice, and teaching.

Our activities

The activities of Al-Iman Center of Iowa include, but are not limited to, the following:

  1. Conduct religious services
  2. Host seminars with renowned teachers of Islamic Studies
  3. Hold engaging events for youth and families
  4. Disseminate Islamic education through our Weekend School program

✦

Over 27 years of service

For over 27 years, Masjid Al-Iman has served the growing Muslim community of Iowa City, Coralville, North Liberty and the surrounding area by providing a place for the worship of Allah (SWT).
